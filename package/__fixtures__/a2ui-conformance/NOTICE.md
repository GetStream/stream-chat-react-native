# A2UI conformance suite

The cases in `core/` and the catalogs in `catalogs/` are the conformance suite of the A2UI
specification, from [a2ui-project/a2ui](https://github.com/a2ui-project/a2ui) at commit `d378d20`,
licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0. `src/a2ui/__tests__/conformance.test.ts` runs them.
Copied as they are: update them by copying a newer commit's `conformance/` over these.
